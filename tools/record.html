Record the phoneme clips

Replaces the synthesised sounds in audio/phonemes/ with a real voice. Record each one, check it, then download. Filenames come out correct — drop them straight into the folder, no code changes.

Say the sound, not the letter's name, and don't add a vowel.

The curriculum manual is specific about this: /buh/, /cuh/, /muh/, /luh/ are wrong, because the child then hears two sounds where there is one (CDM p20). For b d g p t k j ch keep the release as short and clean as you can. For f l m n r s v z sh th ng hold the sound for about half a second. For w and y let it glide — these move, they can't be held still.
